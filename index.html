Why we do this

To explore the uncomfortable intersections of faith and culture through honest conversations that challenge assumptions, encourage critical thinking, and inspire a more thoughtful pursuit of Jesus.

No scripts. No easy answers. Just people who love Jesus, asking the questions out loud that most of us only ask in our heads.

Not another Christian commentary show

We push in both directions.

Plenty of shows exist to defend Christianity against culture. We'd rather be honest about both.

Out there

The culture around us

What's shaping how we think about identity, rest, sex, politics, and suffering, and what Jesus has to say about it.

In here

The Christian culture within us

The assumptions, blind spots, and comfortable habits we've picked up along the way, held up to Scripture.
